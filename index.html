Who we are

A fraternity worth the four years—and the forty after.

Phi Deuteron brings together Kent State men from aviation, engineering, nursing, business, and every corner of campus. We hold one another accountable, serve the community around us, and make a large university feel like home.

Alpha Epsilon Pi is the Jewish fraternity, and our doors are open to every man of good character who shares our values and wants to build something lasting.

Recruitment

Find your place at Kent State.

Rush is free, casual, and open to any Kent State man of good character. No Greek-life experience required.

  1. 01

    Come to an event

    Join us for a cookout, sports night, game night, or dinner at the house.

  2. 02

    Meet the brothers

    Ask real questions and get to know the people behind the letters.

  3. 03

    Talk with leadership

    Discuss academics, time, dues, expectations, and what you want from brotherhood.

  4. 04

    Receive a bid

    If it is a mutual fit, you will be invited to begin new-member education.
