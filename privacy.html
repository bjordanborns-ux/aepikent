Privacy

Alpha Epsilon Pi’s Phi Deuteron Chapter respects the privacy of students, families, alumni, and other visitors to this website.

Information you choose to share

The recruitment form prepares an email in your own email application. Your information is not submitted through or stored by this website. It is sent only if you review and send that email to chapter leadership.

Website analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand general site traffic and performance. Cloudflare may process technical request information under its own privacy terms.

External services

Links and embedded services—including Instagram, Google Calendar, Google Drive, and other third-party resources—are governed by the privacy practices of those providers.

Questions

For questions about this page or how information is handled, email exec@aepikent.org.
