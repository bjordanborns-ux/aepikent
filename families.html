For families

A clear view of membership.

Families deserve straightforward answers about academics, cost, time, safety, and the expectations of fraternity life.

01

Academics first

Members remain students first, with chapter study expectations and peer accountability.

02

Time and finances

Students should understand chapter dues, event commitments, and expectations before accepting membership.

03

Responsible membership

We follow university and national risk-management standards and maintain a strict anti-hazing policy.

04

Open communication

Families are welcome to stop by the chapter house and contact chapter leadership with questions or concerns.

GPA

Academics in action

Highest new member GPA in Kent State IFC

Phi Deuteron has been recognized for earning the highest new member GPA among Kent State’s IFC chapters, supporting our expectation that membership complements—and never replaces—a student’s education.

What to expect

Growth with accountability.

Fraternity membership should complement a student’s education—not compete with it. The strongest experience comes from clear expectations, sound judgment, and honest communication.

Questions worth discussing

  • How will chapter responsibilities fit around classes and work?
  • What are the full financial expectations for the semester?
  • Who should a member or family contact when a concern arises?
  • What leadership, service, and personal-growth opportunities are available?
