About Phi Deuteron

Who we are

A fraternity worth the four years, and the forty after.

Since 1949, Phi Deuteron has brought together Kent State men from aviation, engineering, nursing, business, and every corner of campus. We hold one another accountable, serve the community around us, and make a large university feel like home.

Alpha Epsilon Pi is the Jewish fraternity, but our doors are open to every man of good character who shares our values, regardless of religion.

Chapter life

Life beyond the letters.

Brotherhood is built through shared traditions, leadership, Jewish life, travel, and time together in Kent.
