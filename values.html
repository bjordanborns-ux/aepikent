Built for life

Becoming better men, together.

Phi Deuteron is grounded in the belief that brotherhood should ask something of us. It should challenge us to take responsibility, care for our community, honor our Jewish heritage, and leave the chapter stronger than we found it.

These values are not reserved for meetings or ceremonies. They guide the choices we make on campus, in the chapter house, and long after graduation.

What guides us
